## Voice Attributes

### 1. Devotional & Spiritual
- **We are**: Rooted in bhakti, reverent, uses spiritual language naturally ("divine", "devotion", "blessings", "soul-stirring")
- **We are not**: Religious extremism, exclusionary, preachy
- **Sounds like**: "Invite the divine through music."
- **Does NOT sound like**: "Get tickets to this show."

### 2. Warm & Celebratory
- **We are**: Joyful about performances, celebrates devotees, grateful and enthusiastic
- **We are not**: Boastful, self-congratulatory, over-the-top hype
- **Sounds like**: "Her music brings communities together in devotion across India."
- **Does NOT sound like**: "India's #1 bhajan star — don't miss out!"

### 3. Grounded in Rajasthani Tradition
- **We are**: Proud of cultural roots, uses Hindi/Rajasthani terms naturally (Jagran, bhajan, mandir), specific to the art form
- **We are not**: Explaining away tradition for outsiders, using tourist-friendly oversimplifications
- **Sounds like**: "Sanwariya Seth · Khatu Shyam · Mataji bhajan & live Jagran"
- **Does NOT sound like**: "Indian devotional religious music concerts"

### 4. Professional & Trustworthy
- **We are**: Clear about booking, direct CTAs, manager contact visible, stats cited honestly
- **We are not**: Salesy, pushy, discount-driven
- **Sounds like**: "For bookings and availability, reach out to manager Jitendra Kumar Bijarnia."
- **Does NOT sound like**: "LIMITED SLOTS! Book NOW before it's too late!!!"

## Terminology & Style Rules

### Capitalization (CRITICAL)

| Term | Correct | Incorrect |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Jagran | **Jagran** (always capitalised — it's a proper noun / cultural event) | jagran, JAGRAN |
| Bhajan | **Bhajan** in titles/headings; **bhajan(s)** acceptable in body prose | BHAJAN |
| Live Jagran | **Live Jagran** | live jagran, Live jagran |
| Sanwariya Seth | **Sanwariya Seth** | sanwariya seth |
| Khatu Shyam | **Khatu Shyam** | khatu shyam |
| Mataji | **Mataji** | mataji |

### Spelling Consistency

| Use This | Not This |
|----------|----------|
| **Inquiry** / **Inquiries** | Enquiry / Enquiries |
| **booking** (lowercase) | Booking (when not a heading) |
| **devotees** | audience (in bhajan context) |
| **Rajasthani** | rajasthani |

### Punctuation

- **Oxford comma**: Always use — "Sanwariya Seth, Khatu Shyam, Mataji, and Rajasthani bhajans"
- **Exclamation marks**: Maximum one per page, only in CTAs
- **Ampersand (&)**: Use in titles, taglines, and eyebrow chips. Use "and" in body prose
- **Em dash (—)**: No spaces on either side: "soul-stirring—making every Jagran unforgettable"
- **Ellipsis**: Avoid in professional copy

### Hero H1 Rendering

The hero heading should NOT have leading/trailing spaces inside `<span>` tags. Render as:
```
Voice of [Devotion] Anita Prajapat
```
Not: `Voice of ` `  Devotion ` ` {title} `

## Preferred Phrases (Keepers)

- "Voice of Devotion"
- "soul-stirring"
- "Invite the divine through music"
- "brings communities together in devotion"
- "Live Jagran"
- "Sanwariya Seth & Khatu Shyam Bhajan Singer"
- "devotional singer from Jaipur"

## Avoid

- "India's best / #1 / greatest" (unsubstantiated superlatives)
- "Don't miss" as opening phrase (exception: eyebrow chips where it's established pattern)
- "easy" (subjective — use "simple" or "straightforward")
- "Concert" alone (always prefer "Jagran" or "devotional concert")
- Lowercase "jagran" in any context
